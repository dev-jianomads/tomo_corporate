Our vision

I've spent more than 20 years managing money and raising capital. First as a hedge fund manager, then in private equity and venture.

Early in my career I lost a $20M allocation that I'd been working on for 18 months. Our fund had performed well. The LP had been genuinely interested throughout. But the process had moved too slowly. One meeting every couple of months. Updates going out on a general schedule. Nobody watching for the moment the relationship was ready to accelerate. By the time my head of IR circled back for what should have been the final push, the LP's allocation priorities had shifted. We had not done anything wrong. We just hadn't been watching.

The pattern was always the same. The fund had the story. It had the numbers. What it didn't have was the execution infrastructure to tend every LP relationship, across every cycle, without drifting.

Every tool we tried failed to do what an experienced fundraiser would do. CRMs, relationship platforms, workflow automation. None of them could sit beside you, read the signals, draft the work, and leave the judgment and the relationships to you.

TOMO is my answer. A firm built to give fund managers the execution of the best IR director they've ever worked with, and the access of the best network they've ever had. We take on the quiet, repetitive work that erodes every raise. And we leave you with the part only you can do: the judgment, the relationships, the close.

This is the firm I wish I'd had. I'd like to build it with you.

Geoffrey Chen · Founder & CEO
Former hedge fund CEO · 20 years institutional capital markets
